Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This policy covers the private personal software applications built and operated by Joe Pillatsch, including the personal health and finance dashboard that connects to the Oura API.

Who this software is for

These applications are private and single-user. They are built by one individual for that same individual's own use. They are not offered to the public, have no sign-up, no user accounts, and no other users. No one else's data is ever processed.

What data is accessed

Only the operator's own personal data, retrieved from services the operator already holds an account with and has personally authorized. This may include:

  • Health and biometric data from the Oura API (sleep, readiness, and activity metrics)
  • Personal financial account balances and transactions
  • Personal calendar and journal entries

Where data is stored

Locally, on hardware owned by the operator. The dashboard runs only on the operator's own computer and is not published to the internet or reachable from any external network. There is no hosted server, no cloud database, and no public endpoint.

What is shared

Nothing. No data is sold, rented, shared, transmitted to third parties, or used for advertising. There is no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry, and no third-party processor. Data flows in one direction only: from the source services to local storage.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained locally for as long as the operator finds it useful, and is deleted at the operator's discretion by removing local files. Because there is no server and no other user, there is no data of anyone else to retain or delete.

Third-party services

Where this software reads from external APIs, those services operate under their own privacy policies and their own authorization flows. Access is granted by the operator and can be revoked by the operator at any time from within those services.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Joe Pillatsch via the contact links at joepillatsch.com.
